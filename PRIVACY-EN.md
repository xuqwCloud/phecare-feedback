# PheCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

PheCare is an iPhone and iPad app for parents and caregivers of children with PKU to organize daily food and growth records. This policy explains how PheCare handles information users choose to enter into the app.

## Core Principles

The current version of PheCare uses a local-first design:

- No account registration or sign-in is required
- No advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics is included
- Baby profiles, dietary records, special-formula records, blood values, and growth records are not automatically sent to the developer
- Core calculations, searches, and data organization are performed on the user's device

## Information Entered by the User

Users may choose to store:

- A child's nickname, birth date, gender, weight, and height
- Food intake, food weight, Phe intake, and special-formula records
- Blood Phe, Tyr, Phe/Tyr ratio, test date, and user-entered notes
- Growth measurements
- Custom foods and favorite status
- Dietary management targets and display-unit preferences

This information is used on the device to provide recording, calculation, trend display, editing, import, and export features. The developer does not automatically receive this information through PheCare.

## Local Storage

Core user data is stored in the app's local data container.

PheCare does not provide automatic iCloud or CloudKit synchronization. However, whether an iOS device backup or device migration includes app data depends on the user's Apple Account, device settings, and Apple's system behavior. Such processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Export, Import, and Sharing

PheCare provides data-management actions initiated by the user:

- **Complete data export** may include the baby profile, dietary records, special-formula records, blood values, growth records, custom foods, and relevant settings
- **Complete data import** lets the user select a backup file and restore its contents into the app
- **Food sharing packages** contain only custom food data selected by the user and do not contain a baby profile, dietary records, blood values, or growth records

An exported backup may contain a minor's personal records and is currently saved in a readable data-file format. Do not send a backup to an untrusted person or upload it to a public Issue, forum, or file-sharing page.

The user chooses the export destination through the iOS file picker or share interface. If the user selects iCloud Drive, a third-party storage provider, email, or another service, further processing is governed by that service's privacy policy. The PheCare developer cannot control those external copies.

## Bundled Food Data

PheCare's food reference data is bundled with the app and searched locally. The current version does not automatically transmit food search terms, dietary records, or baby information when the user searches for food.

Nutrition data may come from public databases, consensus materials, manufacturers, or estimates. The app identifies sources and data types where possible.

## Third-Party Services and External Links

PheCare does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

External links in the app or this support repository open in a browser or the appropriate system service and are governed by the destination's own privacy policy.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on the device until the user:

- Deletes an individual record in the app
- Uses a local reset feature such as “Restore Initial State”
- Uninstalls PheCare
- Erases the device or app data through system features

Deleting in-app data does not automatically delete copies previously exported to Files, iCloud Drive, third-party storage, or another destination. Users must delete those copies at their respective locations.

Because the developer does not automatically receive or retain this local data, the developer cannot view, restore, or remotely delete records stored on the user's device.

## Children's Information

PheCare is intended for use by parents or other adult caregivers and is not designed for children to register for or use directly.

The app allows caregivers to store child-related records locally, but that information is not automatically submitted to the developer. Users should not disclose a child's identity or specific records in public feedback, screenshots, or logs.

## Feature Boundaries

PheCare is intended only for household record keeping, numeric conversions, and information organization. It does not provide a diagnosis or generate an individualized dietary plan or special-formula amount from the user's records.

The app displays sourced age-based reference ranges, editable starting values, unit-conversion results, and numeric comparisons with targets entered by the user. The age-based dietary Phe bands and blood Phe record-reference ranges come from the *Consensus Statement on Dietary Treatment and Nutritional Management for Phenylalanine Hydroxylase Deficiency*, *Chinese Journal of Pediatrics*, 2019, Vol. 57, No. 6, pp. 405–409, DOI: `10.3760/cma.j.issn.0578-1310.2019.06.002`. The in-app “References & Calculations” page discloses the current rule-set version and complete calculation methods.

All ranges, starting values, conversions, and trends are for record keeping and information only. They must not be used to independently adjust a dietary plan, special-formula amount, or other personal arrangement. Results may vary by individual, food state, product batch, sample type, and test method. Users should rely on the original laboratory report, product labels, manufacturer information, and the management basis they have independently confirmed. Confirm changes to targets or daily routines with an appropriately qualified professional first.

## Data Security

We minimize data access and transmission within the app's capabilities. Users should also:

- Protect the device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID
- Store exported backup files securely
- Redact names, birth dates, testing organizations, blood values, and other sensitive details before sharing screenshots
- Never upload a complete backup to a public feedback repository

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when PheCare's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.
